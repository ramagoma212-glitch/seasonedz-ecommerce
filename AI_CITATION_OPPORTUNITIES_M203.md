## What was deliberately NOT recommended

- **No paid link-building, guest-post, or PBN-style services** — these risk a manual action and do not match "legitimate" per the brief's own instruction.
- **No directory submission was automated.** Every row above requires a human (the owner) decision, in several cases because it is a genuine commercial decision (marketplace fees) rather than a technical one.
- **No fabricated review platforms or review-seeding services** were researched or recommended — Seasonedz's own real Google Reviews link is already present in `businessInfo.js` and is the only review surface this report treats as legitimate.
