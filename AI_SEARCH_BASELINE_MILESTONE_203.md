## What this does and doesn't show

- **Confirmed:** the site did not surface for any commercial, informational or brand query tested, including its own name, in this tool's results on this date.
- **Not confirmed:** whether this reflects the site's actual Google index status, Bing index status, or ChatGPT Search status specifically — this tool's index coverage is not disclosed, and a thin or young domain can plausibly rank in one engine's index while being absent from another's, or from a secondary aggregator this tool may be drawing from.
- **Plausible contributing factors, not proven causes** (see Competitor/Citation Analysis below for the reasoning): a comparatively new domain, limited inbound citations/backlinks from third-party sites, and no current marketplace or directory presence (Takealot search for "Seasonedz" also returned nothing — see Marketplace Consistency section of the final report).
- This baseline does **not** mean the technical SEO/GEO work in this milestone was wasted — crawlability, structured data and entity clarity are prerequisites for being indexed and cited at all; they do not themselves manufacture backlinks or domain authority, which is the more likely explanation for the current zero-visibility result.
